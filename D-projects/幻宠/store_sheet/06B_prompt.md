NOT cute/chibi, NOT flat cartoon. Keep violence implied, not gory. No realistic celebrity faces. Do not copy GTA or Peaky Blinders logos, fonts, or costumes.

[TOP - THE LOCAL ANCHOR] The upper portion shows a small but active Chicago city map section: dark street grid with some red/orange event markers (shootouts, robberies) actively happening - the local ground truth, smaller than version A but still alive.

[BOTTOM - THE NATIONAL EXPANSION - MAIN FOCUS] The lower two-thirds of the frame is the USA map outline as the MAIN visual: the yellow faction covers a LARGE area spanning multiple cities (Chicago, Detroit, New York, Los Angeles, etc.), prominent city labels, bold marching-route arrows pushing outward in all directions, territory-color patches expanding visibly (yellow = yours, red/blue = rivals). The nationwide expansion is the hero of this version.

[CONNECTION] A glowing bridge-line connects the local Chicago events (top) to the national ambition (bottom), showing cause and effect - local victories fuel national conquest.

[ANCHOR BATTLE] One small real battle scene (tiny figures, muzzle flashes) is anchored in the local map (top) as the ground-truth that the map events correspond to actual gameplay.

[STYLE] Poster-style store image, dual-narrative: the national expansion map and the local event anchor must be obvious in 3 seconds at 180px. Large yellow territory, bold marching arrows, multiple city labels - the growth is unmistakable. Dark map base with glowing markers, warm gold + purple-blue, American comic realism. Realistic gameplay visuals at least 60%.

[RESTRICTIONS] Do NOT make it a pure satellite map or action-less flags. Do NOT copy real police-app branding or UI. Do NOT show unreachable areas. No gore. No realistic celebrity faces. Do not copy GTA/Peaky Blinders logos, fonts, or costumes.
