> 背景：制作人对 06 的想法未完全明确。提供三种地图呈现风格，请分别生成试用，选最贴近制作人意图的。

## 共同点（三版共用）
- 方向：06-A 芝加哥事件密度
- 中文文案：从一个街区，到统一全美
- 英文标题：FROM ONE BLOCK TO ALL AMERICA.
- UI 统一（见每版末段 UI 描述）
- 共同禁区：不用现实警务App品牌/不复制GTA/不血腥/不用明星脸/不展示无法到达区域

## 风格一：实时犯罪地图风（贴近 image47，无品牌）

### AI提示词-EN
Create a Google Play store featured image (1080x1920 vertical) for a mafia SLG mobile game called GTS. Theme: from one block to all America - a live crime map of Chicago with dense real-time events. Style: American comic realism with neon-noir crime-city energy, dark map base on purple-blue glow, glowing event markers, realistic materials, cinematic lighting. NOT cute/chibi, NOT flat cartoon. Keep violence implied, not gory. No realistic celebrity faces. Do not copy GTA or Peaky Blinders logos, fonts, or costumes.

[THE MAP - LIVE AND ALIVE] A large zoomed-in Chicago city map fills most of the frame, drawn as a dark blue-black street grid with glowing details. The map is ALIVE: pulsing red markers for shootouts, orange for robberies, yellow for recruitment - each with small event icons and motion trails, actively happening right now. This is a real-time crime map where things are currently occurring, NOT a static satellite view.

[EVENT DENSITY] The events are dense and layered - clusters of markers in the hot zones, scattered singles elsewhere - showing "where the crime is happening right now" like a command-center data screen.

[EXPANSION HINT] In the upper area, the USA outline appears small, with a yellow faction spreading from one block to three cities, marching-route trails pointing outward - the local events connect to the national ambition.

[ANCHOR BATTLE] One small real battle scene (tiny figures, muzzle flashes) is anchored on the map as the ground-truth that the events correspond to actual gameplay.

[UI] Standard mobile SLG store screenshot UI, fully integrated: top resource bar (pink diamond +3K, silver coin +200K, gold bar +500K+, gear settings, envelope mail, dark-bg white-text rounded chips); top-left circular player avatar with purple border, nickname, pink star level 30, purple fist power number; top-right top-down city minimap with castle base icon; left vertical buttons ACTIVITY / PASS / STARTER with red notification dots; right vertical buttons RANKING / SHOP / OPSS / BAG; bottom-center large title "FROM ONE BLOCK TO ALL AMERICA." in white bold sans-serif uppercase on a dark backdrop, occupying the bottom 15-20%.

[STYLE] Data-map look that is bright and readable: dense live events + one battle anchor must be obvious in 3 seconds at 180px. Dark map base with glowing red/orange/yellow markers on purple-blue, American comic realism. Realistic gameplay visuals at least 60%.

[RESTRICTIONS] Do NOT copy real police-app branding or UI (no real emergency-app logos); borrow only the "event density" expression. Do NOT make it a pure satellite map or action-less flags. Do NOT show unreachable areas. No gore. No realistic celebrity faces. Do not copy GTA/Peaky Blinders logos, fonts, or costumes.
