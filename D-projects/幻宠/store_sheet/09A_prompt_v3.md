Deeper in the same street behind them, their combat is unfolding - the same three characters mid-firefight. The street pavement, the purple-blue night sky, the streetlights all extend continuously from foreground to background. Nothing divides the image into panels.

[FOREGROUND - THE CREW] The cowgirl hero stands centered and closest to camera, with one disciple on each side slightly behind her. She has her signature cowboy hat and holstered revolver. The two disciples have distinct looks (different outfits, weapons, body types). Small glowing labels float near each: FIRE, BUSINESS, SUPPORT. They look confident, ready.

[DEEPER IN THE SAME STREET] Further back in the same continuous street scene, the same three characters appear mid-combat - executing a team finishing move against rival gangsters, muzzle flashes and golden sparks lighting the night. This is the same crew, same street, same moment - just deeper in the composition.

[BACKGROUND] One uninterrupted purple-blue night sky spans the entire frame. One street. One scene.

[UI] Standard mobile SLG store screenshot UI, fully integrated: top resource bar (pink diamond +3K, silver coin +200K, gold bar +500K+, gear settings, envelope mail, dark-bg white-text rounded chips); top-left circular player avatar with purple border, nickname, pink star level 30, purple fist power number; top-right top-down city minimap with castle base icon; left vertical buttons ACTIVITY / PASS / STARTER with red notification dots; right vertical buttons RANKING / SHOP / OPSS / BAG; bottom-center large title "BUILD A CREW FOR EVERY HUSTLE." in white bold sans-serif uppercase on a dark backdrop, occupying the bottom 15-20%.

[STYLE] One continuous composition with natural foreground-to-background depth, like a movie poster. American comic realism, warm gold on purple-blue night. NOT a gacha screen. NOT a split panel collage.

[RESTRICTIONS] Do NOT divide the image into panels, sections, or split screens with lines or hard edges. The entire image is one scene. Do NOT make it a gacha pull screen. This is the cowgirl's LAST strong appearance. No marijuana or drugs. No gore. No realistic celebrity faces. Do not copy GTA/Peaky Blinders.
